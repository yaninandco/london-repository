FROM DAMAGED TO REBUILT

The Final Step in Restoration

Mitigation and cleanup stop the damage from spreading. Renovation and rebuild work is what brings your property back to full function — repairing structure, replacing what couldn't be saved and rebuilding what was lost.

We work from the same damage assessment used for mitigation, so every repair reflects what actually happened to your property.

WHAT WE REBUILD

What We Repair & Rebuild

  • Structural repair and framing
  • Kitchen repair and rebuild after water or fire damage
  • Bathroom repair and rebuild
  • Basement rebuild following flooding
  • Drywall, insulation and interior finishes
  • Flooring replacement and repair
  • Painting and final finishing
  • Full room or whole-property rebuilds when damage is extensive

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

How This Differs From a Standard Renovation

A typical renovation starts with a blank slate. A rebuild after damage follows a different process, shaped by the scope of loss, current code requirements and often an active insurance claim.

  • Repairs follow the scope of the actual damage, not just design preferences
  • Rebuilt areas are brought up to current code requirements
  • We coordinate directly with insurance adjusters where needed
  • Materials and finishes are matched to the rest of the property where possible
  • Structural areas are checked for integrity before finishing work begins

OUR PROCESS
