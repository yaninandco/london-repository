WHAT IS ASBESTOS?

What Is Asbestos and Why It Matters

Asbestos is a mineral fibre once widely used for fireproofing and insulation. Many properties built before the 1990s still contain it in flooring, insulation or ceiling materials.

Undisturbed asbestos is generally not a concern. The risk comes from disturbing it during renovation, demolition or damage, which releases fibres into the air.

WHY IT MATTERS

Health Risks of Asbestos Exposure

Inhaling airborne asbestos fibres has been linked to serious health conditions, which is why testing and removal should always be handled by trained professionals.

  • Asbestosis (scarring of lung tissue)
  • Mesothelioma
  • Lung cancer
  • Other respiratory complications

WHERE IT'S FOUND

Where Asbestos Is Commonly Found

Asbestos-containing materials can show up in places you wouldn't expect, especially in properties built or renovated before the 1990s.

  • Attic and wall insulation, including vermiculite
  • Pipe, boiler and duct insulation
  • Vinyl floor tiles and adhesives
  • Textured "popcorn" ceilings
  • Older roofing, siding and cement products
  • Drywall joint compound and plaster

Lab Results & Quote
